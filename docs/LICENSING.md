# Licensing and owner review

The app is 100% free. `LICENSE` is a free proprietary application licence: using an official build for creative work is permitted without a subscription; application redistribution, resale, rebranding or incorporation into another product requires appropriate permission, subject to law and third-party rights. It does not claim user-created output.

This is an owner-review draft, not a formal licence grant from any external party. Confirm the actual legal rights holder/entity and a viable permission contact before company distribution. A public GitHub repository still has GitHub's viewing/forking terms. Neither a licence nor a public source repository can physically prevent copying or create copyright rights that do not exist.

The service and provider terms that applied during development distinguish content ownership from restrictions on licensing, distributing and commercially exploiting output. Supplemental terms may control a particular account. Get written clarification of those obligations before public or company distribution; deleting or reimplementing source does not change them. 
Third-party libraries/fonts/runtimes retain independent licences. Generated notices and the exact installed unmodified Mediabunny package sources/licence accompany builds. App proprietary restrictions do not override MPL 2.0 rights or source obligations, MIT/ISC permissions, font licences or mandatory legal rights. Code signing, company registration, consumer obligations and enforceable jurisdiction-specific licence terms need appropriate owner review.
