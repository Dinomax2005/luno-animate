The app is 100% free. `LICENSE` is a free proprietary application licence: using an official build for creative work is permitted without a subscription; application redistribution, resale, rebranding or incorporation into another product requires appropriate permission, subject to law and third-party rights. It does not claim user-created output.

This is an owner-review draft, not an official licence grant from Arena. Confirm the actual legal rights holder/entity and a viable permission contact before company distribution. A public GitHub repository still has GitHub's viewing/forking terms. Neither a licence nor a public source repository can physically prevent copying or create copyright rights that do not exist.

Arena's general Terms of Use distinguish content ownership in section 3.2 from restrictions on licensing, distributing and commercially exploiting Output in section 5(i). Supplemental terms may control a particular service/account. Get written clarification for Agent Mode and any provider obligations before public/company distribution; source deletion/reimplementation does not change those underlying terms. Official public terms: https://help.arena.ai/articles/5629909088-terms-of-use .

Third-party libraries/fonts/runtimes retain independent licences. Generated notices and the exact installed unmodified Mediabunny package sources/licence accompany builds. App proprietary restrictions do not override MPL 2.0 rights or source obligations, MIT/ISC permissions, font licences or mandatory legal rights. Code signing, company registration, consumer obligations and enforceable jurisdiction-specific EULAs need appropriate owner/legal review.
